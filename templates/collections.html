How to measure

Use a soft measuring tape and keep it comfortable against your skin—not too tight, not too loose.

  • Bust: Measure around the fullest part of your chest, keeping the tape parallel to the floor.
  • Waist: Measure at the narrowest part of your waist, about two fingers above your belly button.
  • Hip: Measure around the fullest part of your hips, passing over the hip bones.
  • Length: Measure from the top of the shoulder down to the desired hem length.

Wash care

Hand wash cold with like colours. Do not bleach. Dry in shade and iron on reverse.
